## Abstract

This paper introduces the theoretical framework of "Harmony Physics," founded on a fundamental idea: the universe is built from discrete units of energy called "AZH Pixels." This pixel is directly extracted from the frequency of the 21-cm hydrogen hyperfine transition and the most precise measurements of the fine-structure constant (α) and the muon magnetic anomaly (g-2) from 2023 to 2025. Using a tensor network with 80 dimensions and a computational base of 4096 = 2¹², it is shown that all subatomic particles (from the electron to the Higgs boson) can be expressed as integer multiples of a "base soliton" with energy 8516.649166 eV. For the first time, this model calculates the production time of the 21-cm photon using quantum fine structure and predicts a value of 10.7 million years. Harmony Physics reproduces the masses of all Standard Model particles with claimed high precision and predicts new particles such as the G* boson with a mass of 8.516 TeV.
